## ۰۱ — بهینه‌سازی عنوان بلاگ

### پرامپت ۱ — تولیدکنندهٔ عنوان قدرتمند
> You are an SEO content strategist. I want to rank for the keyword: `[کلیدواژه]`.
> My target audience is `[مخاطب — مثل بلاگرهای مبتدی، بنیان‌گذاران SaaS، صاحبان کسب‌وکار محلی]`.
> Generate 10 SEO-optimized blog title variations for this keyword. Apply the following rules:
> - Primary keyword must appear in the first 60 characters
> - At least 3 titles must use a number (listicle format)
> - At least 2 must use a "How to" format with a clear outcome
> - At least 2 must use a power word that signals urgency, exclusivity, or transformation
> - Flag which titles have the highest CTR potential and explain why in one sentence each
> Output as a numbered list with CTR notes.

### پرامپت ۲ — ممیزی عنوان رقبا و بازنویسی
> Act as an SEO strategist. Here are the top 5 blog titles currently ranking on Google for the keyword `[کلیدواژه]`:
> 1. `[عنوان ۱]` 2. `[عنوان ۲]` 3. `[عنوان ۳]` 4. `[عنوان ۴]` 5. `[عنوان ۵]`
> Analyze these titles for:
> - Keyword placement patterns
> - Emotional triggers used
> - Format types (listicle, how-to, question, etc.)
> - Missing angles or content gaps
> Then generate 5 original title alternatives that are differentiated from competitors while still targeting the same keyword. Each title must have a unique angle not present in the competitor set. Label each with the angle it's using.

## ۰۲ — نوشتن متا دیسکریپشن

### پرامپت ۱ — نویسندهٔ متای پر-CTR
> You are an SEO copywriter specializing in click-through rate optimization.
> Blog title: `[عنوان]` · Primary keyword: `[کلیدواژه]` · Secondary keyword (optional): `[اختیاری]` · Target audience: `[مخاطب]`
> Write 5 meta description variations for this blog post. Each must:
> - Be between 140–155 characters (not including spaces in count)
> - Include the primary keyword naturally within the first half
> - End with a clear micro-CTA (e.g., "Find out how", "See the full breakdown", "Get started here")
> - Create a curiosity gap or promise a specific outcome
> - NOT use generic phrases like "In this article" or "Learn about"
> After each variation, include the character count and label the primary hook used (curiosity, outcome, urgency, social proof, contrast).

### پرامپت ۲ — ممیزی و اصلاح متا
> You are an SEO conversion specialist. Audit the following existing meta description and rewrite it to maximize CTR.
> Current meta description: `[متای فعلی]` · Target keyword: `[کلیدواژه]` · Page URL slug: `[اسلاگ]`
> Competitor meta descriptions for the same keyword: `[متاهای رقبا]`
> Identify: 1. What is weak or generic about the current version 2. Whether the keyword placement is optimal 3. Whether the CTA is strong enough
> Then produce 3 improved rewrites, ranked by predicted CTR performance. Explain the decision behind each in one line.

## ۰۳ — اوتلاین محتوا بر اساس جستجوی کاربر

### پرامپت ۱ — سازندهٔ اوتلاین مبتنی بر قصد
> You are an SEO strategist and content architect. I want to create a high-ranking blog post targeting this keyword: `[کلیدواژه]`.
> Before building the outline, identify:
> 1. The dominant search intent (informational / commercial / navigational / transactional)
> 2. The content format Google is rewarding for this keyword (listicle, how-to guide, comparison, pillar page, etc.)
> 3. Secondary intents present in this SERP
> Then build a complete, SEO-optimized content outline with:
> - H1 (include primary keyword)
> - H2 sections (minimum 5, each targeting a semantic variation or related subtopic)
> - H3 subsections under each H2 where necessary
> - A "What to include" note under each H2 explaining what the section must cover to satisfy user intent
> - Internal link placement suggestions (flag where to add them)
> - FAQ section at the end targeting PAA (People Also Ask) questions for this keyword
> Format as a structured outline, not a paragraph.

### پرامپت ۲ — اوتلاین تحلیل شکاف SERP
> Act as an SEO content strategist. I'm targeting the keyword `[کلیدواژه]`.
> Here are the top 3 ranking articles for this keyword (paste title + key headings from each):
> Article 1: `[سرتیترها]` · Article 2: `[سرتیترها]` · Article 3: `[سرتیترها]`
> Analyze these for:
> - Topics all three cover (table stakes — must include)
> - Topics only 1–2 cover (opportunity areas)
> - Topics none cover but the audience clearly needs (content gaps)
> Then build a content outline for my article that covers all table stakes AND fills at least 3 content gaps that competitors are missing. Flag the gap sections explicitly. This outline should give me a structural advantage over all three competing articles.

## ۰۴ — ممیزی سئوی داخل صفحه (On-Page)

### پرامپت ۱ — ممیزی کامل سئوی داخل صفحه
> You are a senior SEO auditor. Conduct a full on-page SEO audit of the following content.
> Target keyword: `[کلیدواژه اصلی]` · Secondary keywords: `[کلیدواژههای فرعی یا none]` · Page URL: `[URL یا not published yet]`
> Content to audit: `[محتوای کامل]`
> Audit the following elements and give each a status (✅ Strong / ⚠️ Needs Work / ❌ Critical Issue):
> 1. Title tag — keyword placement and length
> 2. Meta description — CTR optimization and keyword presence
> 3. H1 — clarity, keyword inclusion, uniqueness
> 4. H2/H3 structure — semantic coverage and hierarchy
> 5. Keyword density — primary and LSI keyword distribution
> 6. Introduction — does it address search intent within first 100 words?
> 7. Internal linking — presence and relevance
> 8. Image alt text usage (based on any images mentioned)
> 9. Content depth — does it fully satisfy the searcher's query?
> 10. CTA placement — is there a clear next step for the reader?
> For every ⚠️ or ❌ item, provide the exact fix needed. Not suggestions — fixes.

### پرامپت ۲ — پاس بهینه‌سازی کلیدواژه
> You are an on-page SEO specialist. I want to optimize the following piece of content for the primary keyword `[کلیدواژه]` without keyword stuffing or making it sound unnatural.
> Content: `[محتوای شما]`
> Do the following:
> 1. Identify where the primary keyword is currently placed and whether placement is optimal
> 2. Suggest 5–8 LSI (Latent Semantic Indexing) keywords and NLP-related terms that should appear in this content to strengthen topical authority
> 3. Rewrite the introduction (first 150 words) to naturally include the primary keyword and immediately signal relevance to the searcher's intent
> 4. Suggest 2–3 subheading rewrites that incorporate secondary keywords more effectively
> 5. Add a recommended internal link anchor text suggestion and describe what type of page it should link to
> Output each section as a labeled block. Do not rewrite the entire article — only the specified elements.

## ۰۵ — پیشنهاد لینکسازی داخلی

### پرامپت ۱ — سازندهٔ استراتژی لینک داخلی
> You are an SEO strategist specializing in site architecture and internal linking.
> I am publishing a new article titled: `[عنوان]` · Primary keyword: `[کلیدواژه]` · Topic cluster it belongs to: `[خوشه — مثل Instagram Growth]`
> Here are the other articles currently on my site (paste titles and URLs): `[مقالات موجود]`
> Do the following:
> 1. Identify which existing articles should link TO this new article (incoming internal links) and suggest the exact anchor text to use for each
> 2. Identify which articles this new article should link OUT TO, and where in the content those links should be placed (early, mid, or end) and why
> 3. Flag any orphan link risks — existing articles that currently have no internal links pointing to them that could be fixed using this new article
> 4. Suggest 1 pillar page opportunity if a cluster hub page is missing
> Output as two tables: Incoming Links | Outgoing Links — with columns: Article Title | URL | Anchor Text | Placement.

### پرامپت ۲ — بهینهسازی انکرتکست
> You are an SEO specialist focused on internal link equity and anchor text strategy.
> I want to add internal links to the following article:
> Article title: `[عنوان]` · Primary keyword: `[کلیدواژه]` · Full content: `[محتوا]`
> Pages I want to link to from this article: 1. `[صفحه — URL — کلیدواژه هدف]` 2. … 3. …
> For each target page:
> 1. Find the most contextually relevant section in my content where the link fits naturally
> 2. Write the exact sentence or phrase where the link should be inserted (with anchor text bolded)
> 3. Confirm whether the anchor text is exact match, partial match, or branded — and flag if any anchor text is over-optimized
> Also identify 2 spots in my content where I could add internal links to pages I haven't listed yet — based on topic relevance. Describe what type of content those missing pages should cover.
